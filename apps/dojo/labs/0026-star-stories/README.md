# Lab 0026: six stories, 90 seconds each

`cp stories.start.md stories.md`, read the example, then write stories 1 to 6.

`bash check.sh` reads each story the way an interviewer listens, and checks:

1. **Structure:** Situation, Task, Action, Result, Learned, in that order, none empty.
2. **Length:** at most 90 seconds spoken (225 words at 150 words a minute), and at least 45 seconds (115 words): enough to say something real.
3. **Weight:** the Action is at least 40% of the story; Situation and Task together at most 30%.
4. **Ownership:** in the Action, "I" at least three times, and more often than "we".
5. **Evidence:** the Result contains a number, in digits or in words ("three hundred milliseconds").
6. **No filler:** none of "basically", "kind of", "sort of", "stuff", "things like that", "a lot of", "very".

It also prints how long each story takes out loud. Then read each one aloud with
a timer: the checker estimates, your mouth decides.
