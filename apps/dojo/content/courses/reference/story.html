A STAR story, in 90 seconds

PartTimeSays
Situation~10 swhere, when, what was wrong
Task~10 syour goal and your role
Action~45 swhat I did, step by step, and why
Result~15 swhat changed, with a number
Learned~10 swhat I now do differently
  • “I”, not “we”. Numbers, not adjectives. Decisions with their trade-offs (the follow-up is “why not X?”). No filler.
  • About 150 words a minute out loud: 90 seconds ≈ 225 words.
  • Six stories: the sandbox, the faster query, the idempotent queue, a production bug, a disagreement, a failure.

A design interview, in order

  1. Requirements: functional, and non-functional with numbers. Ask first.
  2. Estimates: rate = users × actions ÷ 86,400 (≈ 105); peak = rate × factor; concurrency = rate × time each stays (Little's law); storage = items × size × days.
  3. API and data model, then the design, following one request end to end.
  4. One part in depth: the judge.
  5. At 100×: "bottleneck → change", following the load: runners, connections, hot reads, isolation.
  6. Trade-offs: "X instead of Y, because Z", and when it would flip.
